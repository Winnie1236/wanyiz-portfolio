# User research 

## Target audience
The target audience for this project is everyday consumers who buy electronic devices and storage products, particularly those who have noticed price increases but do not understand the supply chain reasons behind them. This includes students, gamers, and general tech users aged 18 to 35. I identified representative individuals from my immediate network who fit this profile: someone who uses SD cards regularly, a casual gamer, and a general tech user.

## Interview script

| Goal | Questions to Ask |
|------|------------------|
| Evaluate data visualizations | Did the charts help you understand why prices went up? |
| Identify improvements | Is there anything you would change about them? |
| Assess relevance to audience | Who do you think this story is written for? Does it feel relevant to you? |

## Interview findings

| Questions | Interview 1 (student, non-tech major, early 20s) | Interview 2 (student, STEM major, early 20s) | Interview 3 (student, MISM-BIDA major, early 20s) |
|-----------|--------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Did the charts help you understand why prices went up? | Yes, especially the capacity allocation chart. | Yes, the first chart clearly identified late 2023 as the turning point. | Yes, the capacity chart effectively showed the supply-side shift. |
| Is there anything you would change about them? | The "Price Index" label felt abstract. Suggested showing a specific product price increase instead. | Wanted clarification on whether the capacity percentages are global or manufacturer-specific. | Suggested adding key event annotations such as the ChatGPT launch date. |
| Who do you think this story is written for? Does it feel relevant to you? | Everyday consumers who noticed prices rising but did not know why. Very relatable. | Hardware enthusiasts or tech analysts. Relevant for understanding AI's downstream impact. | People interested in tech economics. Interesting even without a gaming background. |

# Identified changes for Part III

| Research synthesis | Anticipated changes for Part III |
|-------------------|----------------------------------|
| "Price Index" label felt abstract to non-tech users | Add a concrete product price example as annotation on the first chart |
| Capacity chart source was too vague | Add a clearer source note specifying IDC and TrendForce coverage |
| Charts lacked key event markers | Add annotations for major AI milestones such as ChatGPT launch |
| Audience perception varied | Add a clearer opening statement explicitly addressing everyday consumers |
